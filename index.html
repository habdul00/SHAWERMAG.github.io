OUR STORY

It all started with a simple love for authentic, fresh flavors and the joy of sharing food that brings people together. Our shawerma isn’t just a meal — it’s a tradition, carefully crafted with passion and care.

Each wrap is made fresh daily using thinly sliced chicken and steak, marinated to perfection with a secret blend of spices inspired by Middle Eastern heritage. We prepare all our sauces in-house, creating rich, vibrant flavors that complement every bite.

What makes us different? We believe in quality without compromise. From the hand-cut veggies to the freshly baked flatbreads, everything is made with the same love and attention we’d give to our own family’s meal.

What we’re proud of

  • House-made garlic, tahini & hot sauces
  • Palestinian flavors cooked with Charlotte in mind
  • Big portions, no shortcuts, always fresh
  • Catering that actually feels like your favorite food truck pulled up
